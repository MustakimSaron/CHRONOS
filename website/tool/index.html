What
CHRONOS-102
delivers

Real SPHEREx data

Public, calibrated infrared spectral images from NASA's IRSA archive, two visits and six detectors per field.

Blink, scrub, read

Compare the two visits across 102 bands to reveal motion, then read any pixel's spectrum and light curve.

Checked before it counts

Independent tests reject detector ghosts and confirm real motion with Gaia, WISE, NEOWISE and JPL.

The method

A difference-imaging pipeline for motion
